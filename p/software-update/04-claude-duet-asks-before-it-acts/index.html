Software Update No. 04 · Week of Oct. 19, 2026 · All 5 issues

Claude Duet asks before it acts.

Anthropic's Duet works in the background and waits for approval before it sends, pays, signs or deletes. Gmail Live arrived on by default. Check which assistants wait for approval. Also: locked chats in ChatGPT.

As of Oct. 22, 2026 · About a 4-minute read

This week's updates

Duet works while you're away and asks first.

Anthropic opened a research preview of Duet on Oct. 21, an app where Claude checks your connected accounts and works on tasks while you're away, but asks before it sends, pays, signs or deletes. Google turned on Gmail Live, which answers questions about your inbox by voice, by default for organizations with Gemini. OpenAI added a way to hide sensitive chats. Gmail's AI Inbox reaches Scheduled Release domains on Oct. 26, and Anthropic's updated Usage Policy applies from Nov. 12.

Claude Duet · Google Workspace Updates (Gmail Live) · OpenAI release notes · Google Workspace Updates (AI Inbox) · Anthropic usage policy
What changed · Oct. 14 – 22

Six changes this week

  1. ClaudeOct. 21Pro and Max, U.S. only; research preview by waitlist
    Wait

    Duet: a separate app where Claude works as a proactive agent

    Duet flags what needs attention and completes tasks you ask for, including while you're away, on its own virtual machine. It checks your connected accounts every few hours. Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it will do and waits for approval. Its background checks count toward your usage limits, and Anthropic warns it may misread a thread or what's urgent.

    Claude Duet · Claude release notes
  2. GmailOct. 15Paid Workspace with Gemini; U.S., English; iOS and Android
    Check settings

    Gmail Live answers questions about your inbox by voice

    Tap Live in the Gmail search bar and ask about your mail out loud; each answer comes with a card linking to the emails it used. It's on by default for organizations with Gemini in Workspace, and rolls out over up to 15 days from Oct. 15. Google says it respects the existing smart features and personalization admin settings, so admins control it there. Check the source card before you act on a spoken answer.

    Google Workspace Updates (Gmail Live)
  3. ChatGPTOct. 20Free, Go, Plus and Pro; rolling out gradually
    Check settings

    Locked chats hide selected conversations behind a PIN

    Open a chat's more options menu (•••) and select Lock chat. It disappears from chat history, pinned chats and search, and opens with your 4-digit account PIN or device authentication. OpenAI says your retention and model-improvement settings stay unchanged, so a locked chat stays in your account and those settings still apply to it.

    OpenAI release notes
  4. GmailScheduled Release from Oct. 26Paid Workspace; U.S., English; Starter and Education later
    Wait

    AI Inbox pulls to-dos and topic summaries from your email

    AI Inbox suggests to-dos and deadlines from your threads and summarizes long discussions by topic. It first reached Rapid Release domains on Oct. 12; Scheduled Release domains start on Oct. 26, each over up to 15 days. Google says there is no admin control for it, and Starter and Education editions get it in the coming months. Open it from the Gmail menu once it arrives, and treat a suggested deadline as a draft until you've read the thread.

    Google Workspace Updates (AI Inbox)
  5. ChatGPTOct. 16Pro, Business and Enterprise with Space access; rolling out
    Wait

    ChatGPT Slides builds decks your team can edit together

    Ask ChatGPT in Work to create a presentation in Space, from a conversation, files, an outline or an example deck. You can edit text, images, shapes and charts, invite teammates to edit in real time, and export to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides on the web. It's rolling out to eligible users, so it may not have reached you. Use it with work files only if your company allows it.

    OpenAI release notes
  6. PerplexityOct. 19Sales-assisted Enterprise plans
    Check settings

    Enterprise admins can choose which roles use each connector

    Admins on sales-assisted Enterprise plans can now decide which roles can use each connector. If your team connects Perplexity to company systems, ask your admin to match each connector to the roles that need it.

    Perplexity changelog
Before next Thursday
  1. Workspace admins: decide on Gmail Live. It's on by default and respects the smart features and personalization admin settings.
  2. Lock the chats you'd rather not show, and remember your retention settings still apply to them.
  3. Before you join the Duet waitlist, list the apps you'd connect, and leave out any it doesn't need.
  4. Note Nov. 12, when Anthropic's updated Usage Policy takes effect.
