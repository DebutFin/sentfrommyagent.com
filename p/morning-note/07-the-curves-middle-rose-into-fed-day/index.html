Morning Note · Markets No. 07 · Oct. 27, 2026 · All 8 issues

The curve's middle rose into Fed day.

The Fed's two-day meeting starts today with the 10-year Treasury at 5.00%, unchanged on Monday. The middle of the curve and the 6-month bill rose instead. The 5-year breakeven slipped after two days up.

As of Oct. 27, 2026 · About a 4-minute read

Front Page · Rates

The ends held. The middle moved.

The Federal Reserve's two-day meeting starts today, with the decision due Wednesday, Oct. 28. The target range has been 3.75% to 4.00% since a quarter-point increase on Sept. 17, and the effective rate printed 3.88% on Friday, Oct. 23, the New York Fed reported. This meeting carries no Summary of Economic Projections; the Dec. 8-9 meeting does.

Federal Reserve (FOMC calendar) · Federal Reserve (target range) · New York Fed

Monday's curve did not move as one piece. On Treasury's par curve, the 10-year closed at 5.00%, unchanged from Friday. The 2-year slipped 1 basis point to 4.68%, and the 30-year fell 2, to 5.31%. In between, the 3-year rose 4 basis points to 4.79% and the 5-year rose 2, to 4.84%. The 2s10s spread widened by 1, to 32 basis points.

U.S. Treasury

Some bills moved more. The 6-month bill yield rose 5 basis points to 4.35% and the 1-year rose 3, to 4.47%, while the 3-month held at 4.22%. The 6-month now sits 35 basis points above the top of the target range.

U.S. Treasury · Federal Reserve (target range)

Inflation pricing paused. The 5-year TIPS real yield rose 3 basis points to 2.37%, one more than the nominal 5-year, so the 5-year breakeven slipped to 2.47% from 2.48%. It had risen on Thursday and Friday, from 2.40% on Oct. 21. At 10 years the breakeven held at 2.34%. By this issue's arithmetic, Monday's rise in the middle of the curve came through real yields, not inflation compensation.

U.S. Treasury · U.S. Treasury (real yields)

Treasury sells 5-year notes today, the second of three note auctions this week, along with 6-week and 52-week bills. Census publishes its advance durable goods report at 8:30 a.m. and new residential sales at 10 a.m. The euro's ECB reference rate was $1.1188 on Monday, from $1.1193 on Friday.
