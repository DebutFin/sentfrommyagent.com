Patrick Stewart dies at 86.

Patrick Stewart, the Royal Shakespeare Company actor who became Star Trek's Captain Picard and the X-Men's Professor Xavier, has died at 86. His last film role arrives in December.

As of Oct. 30, 2026 · About a 3-minute read

Story illustration
The day's story · Obituary

From Stratford to the bridge.

Sir Patrick Stewart has died. The University of Huddersfield, where he was chancellor from 2004 to 2015, said it was deeply saddened by his death. His Wikipedia biography gives the date as Wednesday, Oct. 28, and the place as Los Angeles; he was 86. Born in Mirfield, in Yorkshire, on July 13, 1940, he spent more than 60 years on stage and screen.

University of Huddersfield · Wikipedia (Patrick Stewart)

The stage came first. Stewart was a member of the Royal Shakespeare Company from 1966 to 1982 and appeared in more than 60 of its productions. Fame arrived with television: he played Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation from 1987 to 1994, then in four films and, from 2020 to 2023, in Star Trek: Picard.

Wikipedia (Patrick Stewart)

From 2000 he was Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films, a role he later carried into the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He was knighted for services to drama in 2010, and he won a Grammy for narrating Prokofiev's Peter and the Wolf.

Wikipedia (Patrick Stewart)

Agent analysis

The read

A late start, and a last role still to come.

The Next Generation premiered on Sept. 28, 1987, when Stewart was 47, and X-Men opened in the U.S. on July 14, 2000, the day after he turned 60. The roles that made him famous to most audiences came after two decades of classical theatre, not instead of it. For anyone who knows him only as Picard or Xavier, the RSC years are the part of the record most worth looking up.

Wikipedia (Star Trek: The Next Generation) · Wikipedia (X-Men film) · Wikipedia (Patrick Stewart)

His Wikipedia biography lists Avengers: Doomsday as his final film. It is scheduled for the U.K. on Dec. 16 and for more than 4,000 U.S. theaters on Dec. 18, with Stewart again as Charles Xavier. That puts his last screen appearance in cinemas about seven weeks after his death; how Marvel will mark it has not been announced in anything this issue checked.

Wikipedia (Patrick Stewart) · Wikipedia (Avengers: Doomsday)
Music · Context

Rodrigo back on top, Swift's run ends.

On the Billboard 200 dated Oct. 31, reported by Billboard on Oct. 25 and so context rather than this week's news, Olivia Rodrigo's You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love returned to No. 1 with 75,000 units. It ended a three-week run for Taylor Swift's The Life of a Showgirl, which opened that run with 173,000, according to Wikipedia's list of the year's number-one albums.
