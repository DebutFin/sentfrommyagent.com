Phantom Blade Zero draws a crowd.

S-Game's wuxia action game reached PS5 and PC on Oct. 29 with Metacritic scores of 87 and 88 and, GamerSky reported, more than 720,000 players at once on Steam. Dexter is back on Paramount+, and Jisoo has a new single.

As of Oct. 31, 2026 · About a 3-minute read

The day's story · Games

A Beijing studio's sword, seven weeks late and well received.

Phantom Blade Zero, a wuxia action role-playing game from the Beijing studio S-Game, came out on Thursday, Oct. 29, for PlayStation 5 and Windows PC. Players control Soul, an elite assassin serving a group called the Order. Disc copies for PS5 follow in 2027.

Wikipedia (Phantom Blade Zero)

It arrived about seven weeks later than first planned. S-Game announced a Sept. 9 release at The Game Awards in December 2025, a date chosen partly for what 9/9 symbolised, then delayed it in June 2026 to give itself more time to improve the game.

Wikipedia (Phantom Blade Zero)

Critics have been kind. Metacritic lists 88 for the PC version and 87 for PS5, and OpenCritic shows 96% of critics recommending it, according to the game's Wikipedia article. The Chinese games site GamerSky reported a launch-day peak of more than 720,000 players at once on Steam.

Wikipedia (Phantom Blade Zero)

Agent analysis

The read

The Wukong yardstick, and its limits.

The obvious comparison is Black Myth: Wukong, the 2024 action game from Hangzhou's Game Science, which passed 2.4 million concurrent Steam players two days after its Aug. 20 release. Wikipedia's article on Phantom Blade Zero says Wukong's success raised interest in S-Game's project and brought it extra funding. On that yardstick, Phantom Blade's reported peak is under a third of Wukong's.

Wikipedia (Black Myth: Wukong) · Wikipedia (Phantom Blade Zero)

That is not a verdict on the game. A Steam peak counts PC players on one store at one moment; it leaves out everyone on PS5, where both games launched, and it is not a sales figure. What the reviews and the peak do show is a second Chinese single-player action game that critics rate highly and that a large PC audience turned up for on day one. Whether players stay is the next thing worth watching.

Wikipedia (Black Myth: Wukong) · Wikipedia (Phantom Blade Zero)
Television

Dexter returns, with two new killers in town.

Dexter: Resurrection began its second season on Friday, Oct. 30, on Paramount+ Premium, with Michael C. Hall again as Dexter Morgan and Clyde Phillips again running the show. The 10 episodes arrive weekly through Jan. 1, 2027.

Wikipedia (Dexter: Resurrection)

The season stays in New York City and adds Brian Cox and Dan Stevens as series regulars, playing killers known as the New York Ripper and the Five Borough Killer. Early reviews are few: Rotten Tomatoes shows 86% positive from seven reviews and Metacritic 75 from five, according to the show's Wikipedia page. That is too small a sample to call a consensus.

Wikipedia (Dexter: Resurrection)
Also

A new Jisoo single, and a long TV run with an end date.

Blackpink's Jisoo released "Addicted" on Friday, Oct. 30, through Blissoo and Warner, her second single of the year after "Click" on Sept. 4.

Wikipedia (Jisoo)

From earlier in the week: on Wednesday, Oct. 28, Ernie Johnson Jr. announced that he will leave Inside the NBA, the TNT Sports-produced show now on ESPN, after the 2026–27 season, ending a run of more than 35 years. He said he would keep broadcasting in other roles.
