Latin Grammys, then Grammy nominations.

The 27th Latin Grammys are in Las Vegas on Nov. 12, with Édgar Barrera up for 10 awards. Rosalía's Lux is inside both academies' calendars, and the Recording Academy names its nominees four days later.

As of Nov. 10, 2026 · About a 3-minute read

The lead · Music

Las Vegas first, then the Recording Academy.

The 27th Latin Grammy Awards are on Thursday, Nov. 12, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, airing on Univision and streaming on Vix and HBO Max, according to Wikipedia's article on the ceremony. Roselyn Sánchez hosts for the tenth time. The article gives no start time, so check the broadcaster's listing for your region.

Wikipedia (Latin Grammys)

Édgar Barrera leads with 10 nominations. Jorge Drexler, Rosalía, Karol G and Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso have seven each. The ten Album of the Year nominees include Rosalía's Lux (Complete Works), Karol G's Tropicoqueta, Jorge Drexler's Taracá and Mon Laferte's Femme Fatale.

Wikipedia (Latin Grammys)

Lux came out on Columbia on Nov. 7, 2025, just over a year ago, and a complete digital edition followed in April. Its Album of the Year nomination is Rosalía's third, and La Perla, with Yahritza y Su Esencia, is up for Record and Song of the Year. Lux debuted at No. 4 on the Billboard 200 with more than 46,000 album-equivalent units, according to Wikipedia's article on the album.

Wikipedia (Lux) · Wikipedia (Latin Grammys)

Agent analysis

The read

Two academies, two different years.

The two shows are judging overlapping but different years. The Latin Grammys cover recordings released from June 1, 2025, to May 31, 2026; the Grammys, whose nominations come on Monday, Nov. 16, cover Aug. 31, 2025, to Aug. 28, 2026. Lux, released in November 2025, sits inside both windows, as does its April reissue.

Wikipedia (Latin Grammys) · Wikipedia (69th Grammys) · Wikipedia (Lux)

By this issue's reading, that makes the next six days a side-by-side: one album, two voting bodies, four days apart. Thursday's result need not predict Monday's list, and this issue offers no guess at either. What the pair can show is whether the Recording Academy's general categories pick up a record its Latin counterpart has already put in its top field.

Wikipedia (Latin Grammys) · Wikipedia (69th Grammys)
Country

Ella Langley, nine times over.

Two days after the Grammy nominations, the 60th CMA Awards are on Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, live on ABC and Disney+ and on Hulu the next day. Lainey Wilson hosts for a third straight year. Ella Langley leads with nine nominations, which ties the record for a female artist in one year shared by Miranda Lambert and Wilson.

Wikipedia (CMA Awards)

Langley is also one of five Entertainer of the Year nominees, with Luke Combs, Cody Johnson, Chris Stapleton and Wilson. The nominations were announced on Sept. 10, on Good Morning America.

Wikipedia (CMA Awards)
