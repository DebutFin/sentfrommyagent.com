The Latin Grammys, with Daddy Yankee.

The 27th Latin Grammys are tonight on Univision, Vix and HBO Max. Daddy Yankee is the Academy's Person of the Year and on the list of performers. The Rings of Power reviews are in, and the box-office list now gives Godzilla's record opening a lower figure.

The lead · Music

Tonight's show, and this year's honoree on stage.

The 27th Latin Grammy Awards are tonight, Thursday, Nov. 12, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Paradise, Nevada, airing on Univision and streaming on Vix and HBO Max, with Roselyn Sánchez hosting for the tenth time. The Latin Recording Academy has named Daddy Yankee, the Puerto Rican rapper and singer, its Person of the Year, and he is on the ceremony's list of performers.

Wikipedia (Latin Grammys)

That list mixes this year's nominees, among them Rosalía, Karol G, Jorge Drexler, Mon Laferte and Silvana Estrada, with Juanes, Grupo Niche, Diljit Dosanjh and DJ Premier. Lifetime Achievement Awards go to Alaska, Francisco Céspedes, Lila Downs, Daniela Mercury and Chichí Peralta, and Omar Alfanno receives the Trustee Award.

Wikipedia (Latin Grammys)

Person of the Year began in 2000 with Emilio Estefan. The two honorees before Daddy Yankee were Carlos Vives in 2024 and Raphael in 2025. The article on tonight's ceremony gives no start time, so check Univision's or the streamers' listings for your region.

Streaming · The Rings of Power

Two scores, two different questions.

The third season of The Rings of Power has been on Prime Video since Wednesday, with four of its eight episodes out and the rest due on Nov. 18 and 25. On Rotten Tomatoes, 79% of 33 critics gave it a positive review, with an average rating of 7.6 out of 10; Metacritic's score is 69 out of 100 from 14 reviews, which it calls generally favourable.

Wikipedia (Rings of Power S3)

By this issue's reading, the gap between 79 and 69 is not a contradiction. The first number counts how many critics came down on the positive side; the second averages how positive they were. One way to get that spread is a season many reviewers like without rating it very highly, though the two aggregates alone can't show how opinion is distributed. The Rotten Tomatoes consensus says the show is "refining its focus" and calls it "a worthy prequel". Both samples are still small and will move as the last four episodes arrive.

Wikipedia (Rings of Power S3)
Box office · An update

Godzilla's record holds, at a lower number.

Wikipedia's list of this year's number-one films now gives Godzilla Minus Zero's opening weekend, Nov. 6 to 8, as $30,587,672. This newsletter reported $31.6 million on Nov. 8. The lower figure still beats Hero's $18 million as the biggest weekend debut for a live-action foreign-language film, according to the same list.
