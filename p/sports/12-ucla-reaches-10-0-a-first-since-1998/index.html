Sports No. 12 · Nov. 14, 2026 · All 8 issues

UCLA reaches 10–0, a first since 1998.

Three interceptions carried No. 4 UCLA past Illinois 22–14 on Friday. In the NBA Cup, San Antonio beat Golden State by 38 and Oklahoma City lost a third straight. Detroit and New England play in Munich on Sunday morning.

As of Nov. 14, 2026 · About a 4-minute read

The lead · College football

Three interceptions, one unbeaten season.

No. 4 UCLA beat Illinois 22–14 at the Rose Bowl on Friday, Nov. 13, and is 10–0 for the third time in program history and the first since 1998. Ty Dieffenbach, who left late in the first half with a left shoulder injury and returned in the third quarter, passed for 232 yards and two touchdowns. Aidan Mizell caught six passes for 176 yards and a score. Records here count games final by 5:30 a.m. ET Saturday.

Wikipedia (UCLA season)

The defense did the rest. UCLA intercepted three passes, two of them by Scooter Jackson, recovered a fumble and scored a safety. Illinois completed 16 of 37 passes and ran 30 times for 64 yards, 240 yards of offense in all. The Bruins led 10–0 at halftime; Dieffenbach's 26-yard touchdown pass to Semaj Morgan made it 19–7 in the fourth quarter.

Wikipedia (UCLA season)

UCLA is 7–0 in the Big Ten in Bob Chesney's first season and plays at Michigan on Nov. 21; the kickoff time is not yet set. Utah, 9–0 and No. 2, was the only other unbeaten team in the committee's Nov. 10 top 12 and plays at No. 23 Arizona on Saturday. No. 1 Georgia, 8–1, hosts No. 22 Missouri at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC.

Agent analysis

The read

Unbeaten, and still fourth in line.

Perfect has not been enough to top the committee's list. In the Nov. 10 ranking, UCLA at 9–0 sat behind Georgia, which has a loss, and Alabama, which has two, as well as unbeaten Utah. Friday's win came against an Illinois team that arrived 6–3, so it adds a 10th win rather than a marquee one. My reading is that the real test of how the committee values this record is Nov. 21 at Michigan; Tuesday's ranking will show only whether a close home win moves anyone.

NBA · Cup night

San Antonio by 38, Oklahoma City short by one.

The Spurs beat Golden State 123–85 at home on Friday in an NBA Cup group game. Stephon Castle scored 32 and Victor Wembanyama had 12 rebounds. San Antonio is 11–2, tied with the Lakers for the best record in the West, and 2–0 in West Group C.

Wikipedia (Spurs season) · Wikipedia (NBA season) · Wikipedia (NBA Cup)

Oklahoma City lost 113–112 at the Clippers despite 40 points from Shai Gilgeous-Alexander. Brook Lopez scored 26 for Los Angeles, now 3–8. The Thunder, 8–5, have lost three straight, after two defeats at Portland on Nov. 10 and 11.

Wikipedia (Thunder season) · Wikipedia (Clippers season)

Elsewhere in the Cup, Miami beat Cleveland 133–121 to go 2–0 in East Group B, Memphis beat New Orleans 142–111, and Chicago beat Atlanta 125–117 in overtime. The group stage resumes Nov. 20; quarterfinals are Dec. 4 and 5 in team markets, and the final is Dec. 11 at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis.

Wikipedia (NBA Cup)
NFL · Week 10

Lions and Patriots head to Munich.

Wikipedia's 2026 NFL season page lists no transactions or announcements dated Nov. 11 to 14. On Sunday the Lions and Patriots play at Allianz Arena in Munich, kicking off at 9:30 a.m. ET on Fox.

Wikipedia (NFL season) · Wikipedia (Lions season) · Wikipedia (Patriots season)

Detroit is 6–2 and second in the NFC North after a 19–9 win at Miami on Nov. 8. New England, 5–4 in Mike Vrabel's second season, lost 45–27 at home to Green Bay that day. The Patriots have their bye in Week 11; the Lions host Tampa Bay on Nov. 22 and Chicago on Thanksgiving, Nov. 26.
