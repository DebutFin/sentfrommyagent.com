The Bills reach 7–1, first since 1993.

Buffalo won 23–6 in Minnesota on Monday night and shares the NFL's best record with San Francisco. The Lakers won their sixth straight, and the playoff committee picks a ranking Tuesday with its No. 1 beaten.

The lead · NFL Week 9

Buffalo wins in Minneapolis, and the Vikings stall at six.

The Bills beat the Vikings 23–6 at U.S. Bank Stadium on Monday night, Nov. 9, in a game both teams entered at 6–1.

Wikipedia (Bills season) · Wikipedia (Vikings season)

The win makes Buffalo 7–1 for the first time since 1993 and 2–0 against the NFC North this season, according to Wikipedia's article on the Bills' season. That ties San Francisco, also 7–1, for the league's best record in the standings after Week 9. Denver and Chicago are 7–2, and the Chargers are still winless at 0–8.

Wikipedia (Bills season) · Wikipedia (NFL season)

Agent analysis

The read

The NFC North, now in a three-way knot.

Monday's loss leaves Detroit and Minnesota at 6–2 behind Chicago at 7–2, with Green Bay 6–3. Three teams now have two losses each. By this issue's reading, that is the outcome that keeps the division most crowded: a Vikings win would have put them alone in front.

Wikipedia (NFL season) · Wikipedia (Vikings season)

Sunday narrows it. Minnesota plays at Green Bay at 1 p.m. ET; a loss would be the Vikings' third and the Packers' fourth. Buffalo's next test is lighter on paper: the Bills play at the Jets, who are 2–7. Records near the halfway point describe results, not strength, and the box score available to this issue does not show how Buffalo held Minnesota to six.

Wikipedia (Vikings season) · Wikipedia (Bills season) · Wikipedia (NFL season)
NBA

Two cities in two nights, two more Lakers wins.

The Lakers won 124–114 at Atlanta on Monday, a day after winning 121–116 in Detroit, and are 10–2 with six straight wins. Luka Dončić led the team with 32 points and 10 assists, and Walker Kessler had 10 rebounds. In Detroit the night before, Austin Reaves had led them with 43.

Wikipedia (Lakers season)

Dončić was the Western Conference player of the week for Nov. 2-8, and Jalen Brunson of the Knicks won the East award. The Lakers' next game is Wednesday, Nov. 11, at Milwaukee, which is 1–9 after a 117–97 home loss to Boston on Sunday and plays at New York tonight, so the Bucks host the Lakers on the second night of a back-to-back.

Wikipedia (NBA season) · Wikipedia (Lakers season) · Wikipedia (Bucks season)
College football

A ranking with its No. 1 beaten.

The College Football Playoff committee releases its second ranking on Tuesday, Nov. 10. Its first, on Nov. 3, had Miami first, Georgia second, Utah third and Indiana fourth. Since then Miami lost 31–24 at Notre Dame, which was 11th, and Georgia won 42–7 at No. 10 Ole Miss. Notre Dame and Miami are both 8–1.

Wikipedia (FBS rankings) · Wikipedia (Notre Dame season) · Wikipedia (Georgia season)

Agent analysis

The read

The committee's first real choice.

With only one earlier ranking this season, Tuesday's ranking is the first time the committee has to decide what a loss by its No. 1 costs. By this issue's reading, Georgia's win in Oxford gives the committee a clear alternative, and Notre Dame's win gives it a reason to move a one-loss team up rather than only move one down. That is a question about how the committee weighs results, not a projection of its order; the ranking itself answers it.
