Sports No. 11 · Nov. 13, 2026 · All 8 issues

Cunningham scores 52 in two overtimes.

Detroit won 144–140 at Washington on Thursday behind a career-high 52 from Cade Cunningham. The Commanders beat the Giants 24–21 with 57 seconds left, and unbeaten UCLA hosts Illinois tonight.

As of Nov. 13, 2026 · About a 3-minute read

The lead · NBA

Fifty-two points, a new career high.

The Detroit Pistons won 144–140 in double overtime at Washington on Thursday, Nov. 12, and Cade Cunningham scored 52 points with 11 assists and five rebounds. His Wikipedia biography lists it as his career high; the previous one was 46, also against Washington, on Nov. 10, 2025. Records here count games final by 5:30 a.m. ET Friday.

Wikipedia (Pistons season) · Wikipedia (Cade Cunningham)

Washington had its own big line: Trae Young scored 37 with 14 assists, and Anthony Davis took 15 rebounds, in front of 17,443 at Capital One Arena. The Wizards are 4–9. Detroit is 8–3, first in the Central Division and third in the East, and has won both meetings this month, after a 122–111 home win on Monday.

Wikipedia (Wizards season) · Wikipedia (Pistons season)

Philadelphia beat Toronto 123–112 at home behind 27 from Tyrese Maxey, two nights after losing to Boston by 22. The 76ers are 10–3, a half-game behind the Celtics, who are 10–2 and next play Dallas on Sunday.

Wikipedia (76ers season) · Wikipedia (Celtics season) · Wikipedia (NBA season)
NFL · Thursday night

Washington wins it with 57 seconds left.

The Commanders beat the Giants 24–21 at MetLife Stadium on Thursday. The lead changed hands on every score after a scoreless first quarter. Isaiah Likely's 66-yard catch from J. J. McCarthy put New York up 21–17 with 8:13 left; Jayden Daniels answered with a 32-yard touchdown pass to Stefon Diggs with 0:57 to go, his third touchdown pass of the night. Cam Skattebo ran for both of the Giants' other touchdowns.

Wikipedia (Commanders season)

Washington is 3–5–1 and third in the NFC East. The Giants, 5–3–1, still lead the division, with Dallas second at 4–5 and Philadelphia fourth at 2–7.

Wikipedia (Commanders season) · Wikipedia (Giants season) · Wikipedia (Cowboys season) · Wikipedia (Eagles season)

Agent analysis

The read

The Giants' lead survives the weekend.

By this issue's arithmetic, nothing on Sunday can take the division lead from New York. The Giants do not play again until Nov. 22, against Jacksonville. Dallas hosts San Francisco on Sunday, and even a Cowboys win would leave them 5–5, a .500 record against the Giants' .611 with the tie counted as half a win. The loss matters more for how the lead looks: Thursday was a chance to go further clear of a division where nobody else has a winning record.

College football

UCLA's unbeaten season goes to prime time.

No. 4 UCLA, 9–0 and 6–0 in the Big Ten in Bob Chesney's first season, hosts Illinois, 6–3, at the Rose Bowl tonight at 9 p.m. ET on Fox. The Bruins won 34–20 at Minnesota last Saturday. Utah, the only other unbeaten team in the College Football Playoff committee's Nov. 10 ranking, plays at No. 23 Arizona on Saturday.

Wikipedia (UCLA season) · Wikipedia (Utah season) · Wikipedia (FBS rankings)

No. 3 Alabama, 7–2, plays at Vanderbilt on Saturday; its page does not yet list a kickoff time. No. 1 Georgia, 8–1 after a 42–7 win over Ole Miss on Nov. 7, hosts No. 22 Missouri on Saturday afternoon.
