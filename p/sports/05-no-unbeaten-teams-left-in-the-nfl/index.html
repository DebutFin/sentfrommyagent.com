No unbeaten teams left in the NFL.

Detroit beat Minnesota 19–15 on Sunday, ending the league's last perfect start. Miami survived 38–35 at North Carolina to stay 8–0, and Memphis is the NBA's only unbeaten team at 6–0.

As of Nov. 1, 2026 · About a 3-minute read

The lead · NFL

The last perfect record falls at Ford Field.

Detroit beat Minnesota 19–15 at Ford Field on Sunday, Nov. 1, in the 1 p.m. ET game on Fox. The Vikings arrived 6–0, their first such start since 2009, and as the league's last undefeated team. At 6–1, they leave no unbeaten team in the NFL.

Wikipedia (Vikings season) · Wikipedia (Lions season)

The result tightens the NFC North. Detroit is 5–2, a game behind Minnesota; Green Bay is 5–3 after its Thursday win over Carolina; and Chicago, 5–2, plays at Seattle on Monday night.

Wikipedia (Lions season) · Wikipedia (Packers season) · Wikipedia (Bears season)

At Highmark Stadium, Buffalo beat Baltimore 36–10 to go 6–1. The Bills' season record says the Ravens finished with 144 total yards after quarterback Lamar Jackson and his backup, Tyler Huntley, both left the game injured. This issue found no team statement on either player's condition.

The read · Minnesota

Close wins leave little margin.

Minnesota's six wins were 39–22, 9–3, 23–16, 15–10, 23–13 and 16–10. Five of them came by 10 points or fewer, and no opponent scored more than 22. That is a defence carrying a team, and Sunday followed the pattern: Detroit's 19 stayed below the 22 Green Bay scored in the opener, the most Minnesota has allowed, but the Vikings' offence managed only 15. A record built on close games can turn quickly. One loss doesn't show whether this one will, and the record alone can't say why the offence stalled in Detroit.

College football

Three teams at 8–0, one close call.

No. 1 Miami won 38–35 at North Carolina on Saturday night, Oct. 31. The Hurricanes led 24–21 going into the fourth quarter, and the teams traded 14 points each in it. Miami is 8–0, 6–0 in the ACC.

Wikipedia (Miami season)

The other two unbeaten teams in the AP poll had easier days. Utah won 49–25 at Cincinnati and UCLA shut out Nevada 27–0 in Pasadena; both are 8–0.

Wikipedia (Utah season) · Wikipedia (UCLA season) · Wikipedia (FBS rankings)

Miami's next game is at Notre Dame, 7–1, on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC; UCLA plays at Minnesota the same day. Before then, the College Football Playoff committee publishes its first rankings on Tuesday, Nov. 3.

NBA

Memphis matches its best start.

The Grizzlies beat Sacramento 136–105 at FedExForum on Saturday, Oct. 31, to go 6–0, which their season record says ties the franchise's best start, set in 2014–15. Cameron Boozer scored 27 points with nine rebounds and Cam Spencer had 13 assists. The Kings fell to 0–6.

Wikipedia (Grizzlies season) · Wikipedia (2026–27 NBA season)

Houston took a day to recover from its first loss. A night after losing 116–111 at Dallas, the Rockets beat Oklahoma City 119–98 at Toyota Center, with Jabari Smith Jr. scoring 30. At 6–1, they sit second in the West behind Memphis.
