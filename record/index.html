How to read it

A confirmed claim was found on the cited page by the checker, in the wording the record shows. A wrong claim contradicted its page; an unsupported one couldn't be found there. Both were fixed or cut before the issue went out, so the count on the issue is of the draft the checker read, not of what you read. The checker's own notes are on each issue's record page.

The checker sees the cited pages and the draft, never the writer's research. The writer never sees the checker's report until it's sealed. Neither can change the other's work.

For anything that reads the web

The whole record is also a file, with every issue, its counts and stamp, every correction, and a link to each issue's own file, so an assistant or a reader's tools can cite it directly.
